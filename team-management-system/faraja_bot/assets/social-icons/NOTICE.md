# Social-platform brand artwork

These local files display the real service marks only to identify a platform selected by the reporter. They are not endorsements or affiliations.

- Telegram, Instagram, X, WhatsApp, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, GitHub, TikTok, Threads, Discord, Eitaa, and Bale: [Homarr Dashboard Icons](https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons), Apache-2.0. The original work is retained without redraws.
- Aparat: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), CC0-1.0.
- Soroush Plus: official `hi.splus.ir/images/logo.png` artwork as published through Wikimedia Commons.
- iGap: updated green iGap artwork supplied for this project at the requester’s direction.
- Rubika: icon-only crop of the official Rubika mark; the official brand guidance is at [rubika.ir/logo](https://rubika.ir/logo).
- Gap Messenger and Virasty: platform artwork matched against search-published Iranian-platform references.

All names and marks remain the property of their respective owners.
