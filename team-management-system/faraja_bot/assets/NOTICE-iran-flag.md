# Iran flag artwork

`iran-flag.png` is a local raster rendering of [Flag of Iran (official).svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Iran_(official).svg) from Wikimedia Commons. The source artwork is published there as public domain.

It is used locally so plate previews retain the official green, white, and red bands, emblem, and border details without requiring a third-party network request.
